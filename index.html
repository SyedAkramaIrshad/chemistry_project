How ChemLab Studio works

Build

Add atoms from the library. Click one atom and then another to connect them with the selected bond type. Drag atoms to refine the structure.

Inspect

Select any atom to see its typical valence, current bond order, and whether the simplified model considers it under-bonded or over-bonded.

React

Build one molecule, add it to the reaction lab, clear the canvas, then build the next reactant. The app recognizes a small set of classroom reactions and displays the balanced equation, reaction family, and typical conditions.

Safety boundary

The site is for conceptual learning only. It does not provide experimental quantities, procedural steps, or operational laboratory guidance.
